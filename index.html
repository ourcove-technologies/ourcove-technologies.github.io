About us

Ourcove Technologies LLC is an independent software company based in California. We design and develop mobile applications focused on privacy, simplicity and meaningful connection.

Our first product, CoveFolk, gives people a quiet, invite-only space to share life with the circles that matter most. Over time, we plan to extend that experience with keepsakes such as printed photo books.
